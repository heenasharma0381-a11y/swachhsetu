# ♻️ SwachhSetu AI

**AI-powered smart sanitation and cleanliness platform for cleaner, healthier communities.**

SwachhSetu AI aims to simplify waste reporting, improve sanitation monitoring, and encourage citizens to actively participate in keeping their surroundings clean.

## ✨ Features

- **AI-Powered Waste Detection** – Analyze uploaded images to identify garbage and sanitation-related issues.
- **Smart Complaint Reporting** – Report cleanliness issues with images and location details.
- **Location-Based Monitoring** – Identify and visualize sanitation problems in affected areas.
- **Intelligent Prioritization** – Prioritize complaints according to severity and urgency.
- **Complaint Tracking** – Monitor complaints from submission to resolution.
- **Waste Hotspot Identification** – Identify areas with recurring garbage accumulation.
- **Admin Dashboard** – Manage complaints, monitor reports, and track resolution progress.
- **Community Participation** – Encourage citizens to contribute towards cleaner surroundings.
- **Cleanliness Insights** – Generate insights into recurring sanitation problems and cleanliness trends.

## 🎯 USP

**Turning garbage complaints into intelligent, location-based, actionable sanitation insights.**

## 🌱 Impact

Promoting cleaner surroundings, healthier communities, responsible waste management, and transparent sanitation monitoring.
